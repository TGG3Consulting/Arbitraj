The delisting schedule is as follows:

Deposit

  • The deposit service for the aforementioned tokens will remain closed at 8:00 on November 3, 2026 (UTC).

Spot Trading Bot

  • KuCoin Trading Bot will delist the aforementioned tokens at 6:00 on November 5, 2026 (UTC), including Spot Grid, AI Spot Trend, Spot Martingale, Infinity Grid, DCA, Spot Grid AI Plus and Smart Rebalance. Users are advised to shut down the running Trading Bots before the time above. If the user fails to close the bot before the time above, the system will automatically shut down the relevant trading bot for the user.

Spot

  • The spot trading pairs of the aforementioned tokens will be delisted at 8:00 on November 5, 2026 (UTC). Users are strongly advised to update or cancel their spot trading orders prior to the time above to avoid potential losses. All trade orders will be automatically removed after trading ceases in each respective trading pair.

Withdrawal

  • The withdrawal service for the aforementioned tokens will be closed at 8:00 on December 7, 2026 (UTC). If you are currently holding any such token, please withdraw them before the closing time. 

  • Notwithstanding the foregoing, KuCoin may close the withdrawal service before the scheduled deadline, due to any project-related issues (including but not limited to the cessation of on-chain activities such as block generation and fund transfers). To the extent permitted by applicable law, KuCoin shall not be liable for any losses arising therefrom.

  • Where a delisted token remains in your account after its withdrawal service closes, KuCoin may convert it into stablecoins on your behalf; Such conversion is not guaranteed,and market conditions may cause the value of a delisted token to fall sharply or become worthless.

  • We strongly advise making withdrawals at your earliest convenience.
